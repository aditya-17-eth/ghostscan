# Frontend identity · November 2026

Reference direction: Umano's type-led composition and contextual navigation, Matveyan's sculptural focal point and restrained monochrome structure. The Kerimov Awwwards URL was inaccessible during review; no claim of a full review of that reference is made.

Revised palette: black #0B0D0A, mineral paper #E6EADF, signal green #B7EE91, reference red #E25449, boundary #2C3528. Space Grotesk carries display typography and the wordmark; Manrope carries interface copy. Both are served locally with OFL licenses in public/fonts.

Layout: a spacious typographic homepage with a geometric scanning-aperture sculpture, then a direct handoff into the dense investigation workspace. The custom geometric scan SVG is reused in navigation and the favicon. No third-party imagery or remote font requests.

The hero is the one bold visual moment. Features use navigable rows rather than repeated marketing cards. Mobile gets an explicit expandable menu, and terminal section links have stable hash destinations. The homepage starts no worker or blockchain requests. Entering the terminal retains the original Anon-RPC initialization and dispatch behavior.

Also addressed: home navigation from the terminal, local typography, share metadata, skip links, responsive layout, neutral styling when private mode is selected but unavailable, and updated browser-test entry routes. No synthetic connection status or fabricated chain activity was added.
